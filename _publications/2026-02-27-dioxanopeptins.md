A genome-mining strategy centered on polyunsaturated fatty acid synthases, followed by NRPS co-localization, identified the dxp biosynthetic gene-cluster family. The work connects this family to dioxanopeptins, antibacterial lipopeptides with a pyruvyl-derived 1,3-dioxane motif.

Hao Xiang performed biosynthetic gene-cluster analysis. The accompanying public repository provides the genome-mining workflow.
