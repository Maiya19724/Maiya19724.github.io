This review organizes antimicrobial natural-product discovery along two dimensions: chemical novelty and engineering accessibility. The resulting four-quadrant framework relates discovery problems to synthetic-biology strategies, from improving established pathways to finding biochemical entry points into unfamiliar scaffolds.
